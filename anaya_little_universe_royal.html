I hope this friendship brings you smiles, comfort, silly moments, and the freedom to be your real self, without pressure to be anything other than you.

For your seventeenth year, I wish you quiet confidence, beautiful surprises, dreams that feel possible, and a future that makes you proud of the person you're becoming. More than anything, I hope you feel loved by life—not just on your birthday, but on all the random days in between, too.

Keep your light, Anaya. The world is big, and your story is only beginning. ♡
